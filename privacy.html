ClipVault Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ClipVault handles information when users connect accounts and manage short-form video content.

Information We Process

ClipVault may process:

  • Basic profile information supplied by a connected platform, such as a display name and account identifier.
  • Authorization tokens required to connect an account.
  • Videos, captions, publishing settings, and related metadata selected by the user.
  • Technical information needed to operate and troubleshoot the application.

How Information Is Used

Information is used to connect authorized accounts, display account information, organize videos, prepare captions, and perform publishing actions requested by the user.

Sharing of Information

ClipVault does not sell personal information. Information is sent to a connected platform only when necessary to provide a feature requested by the user, such as account connection or video upload.

Storage and Security

ClipVault stores application information locally on the user's device. Sensitive credentials are stored using protected system storage when available. No security method can guarantee complete protection.

Third-Party Services

Connected services, including TikTok, process information according to their own privacy policies and terms. ClipVault is not affiliated with or endorsed by TikTok.

User Choices and Data Deletion

Users can disconnect connected accounts and revoke platform access. Requests or questions concerning ClipVault data may be submitted through the support link below.

Children

ClipVault is not intended for children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as ClipVault changes. The effective date above will be updated when material changes are made.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests can be submitted through the ClipVault support page.
