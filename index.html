ClipVault

Organize, review, and publish short-form video content.

ClipVault is a macOS content-management application that helps creators organize, edit, review, and publish short-form videos they own or have permission to use.

How ClipVault Works

  1. The user reviews and selects a video inside ClipVault.
  2. The user connects their own authorized social-media account.
  3. The user reviews the video, caption, and publishing settings.
  4. The selected video is sent to the connected account for publishing or final review.

User Control and Safety

ClipVault does not ask users to provide their social-media passwords. Account access is authorized through each platform's official sign-in process. Users control which videos are selected and can disconnect their accounts.

Content Rights

Users may only process or publish videos they own or have permission to use. ClipVault does not grant users ownership of third-party content.
